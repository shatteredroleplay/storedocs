# Casino

A complete casino for FiveM: table games, slot machines, horse racing, the lucky wheel and a full admin panel. Built for the GTA Online casino interior, and works on any framework through a bridge system.

## Features

* **Table games:** blackjack, American roulette, baccarat (punto banco) and Texas Hold'em poker between players.
* **Slot machines:** ten slot games, switchable on every GTA casino slot prop.
* **Horse racing:** one shared race for the whole casino, played at the betting terminals and shown on the big screen.
* **Lucky wheel:** one free spin per character per day, with chips, cash, items and a car as prizes.
* **Cashier:** buy and sell chips for cash or bank money.
* **House bankroll:** every bet goes into a shared pool and every win is paid from it, with table limits that follow the pool.
* **Admin panel:** dashboard, per-game statistics, player management, rigging, economy, settings and an audit log.
* **Atmosphere:** crowd sound, the animated video wall, a map blip and a chip tracker next to the minimap.
* **Bridges:** qbx, qb, esx, ox, nd and standalone frameworks, plus most popular inventories, target and notify systems.
* **Locales:** English and Swedish included.

| Requirement | Notes |
